## Canadian nautical sources

Light signal details come from the Canadian Coast Guard Atlantic *List of Lights, Buoys and Fog Signals*, reproduced non-commercially with attribution. These files are not covered by the MIT licence: see [DATA-LICENSE.md](DATA-LICENSE.md). Which lights sweep is a best guess from tower descriptions, and lighthouse beams are approximate. [CCG List of Lights](https://notmar.gc.ca/list-lights) · [Canada.ca terms](https://www.canada.ca/en/transparency/terms.html)

The hand-traced traffic separation overlay from a Canadian Hydrographic Service chart preview is excluded. Displayed separation features come from the separate OpenStreetMap Overpass build. [OSM seamark data licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
